## What I do

I spend a lot of time thinking about how things scale — not just technically, but structurally. How does a product reach people without a team? How does a process run without someone watching it? These questions shape how I build.

In practice that means connecting large language models to real business processes and shipping the result to production: content pipelines, autonomous agents, marketplace integrations, and the payment infrastructure that makes them earn.

I'm an **integrator**, not an ML researcher. I *use* models; I don't train them. That line is deliberate, and I'd rather state it up front than waste anyone's time.

Everything below is live. I designed, built, deployed, and operate each one myself — no team, no handoff.
